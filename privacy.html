Who we are

This website (weareunbthrd.com) is run by UNBTHRD, based in Brussels, Belgium. For any question about your personal data, write to unbthrd.contact@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message, to answer you.
  • Let’s Work form (UNBTHRD Studio): your name, email, company, social media, the services you are interested in, your project details, budget range and preferred start date, to answer your request and prepare a proposal.
  • Newsletter and “Get notified”: your email address, to send you news about our events, workshops and Academy sessions. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We only use this information for these purposes. We do not sell it or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Legal basis

We process form messages to answer your request or to take steps before a possible agreement with you, and newsletter sign-ups on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Services we use

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the website forms to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this site. Loading them sends your IP address to Google.
  • Our email inboxes are hosted by Google (Gmail / Google Workspace).

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards they put in place for that.

How long we keep it

We keep messages as long as needed to handle your request and follow up on it, and newsletter addresses until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or receive a copy of it, by writing to unbthrd.contact@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
