Who are we?

UNBTHRD is an international platform focused on helping people discover, awaken, develop and nurture their creativity.

Through events, workshops, activities, educational and digital content we reinforce networking and create communities.

Whether you are an aspiring or established creative, passionate about creativity and entrepreneurship or simply looking for self-development & a like-minded community, UNBTHRD is the place for you to discover your creative self!

Our mission

To educate creatives on the importance of their unique calling and further exploration of their creative purpose through social media, mentoring and other academic tools. So far we've had events in Brussels, Paris & Dubai.

Our vision

To inspire creatives internationally through our events, content & academic tools that will edify and guide them to find and enhance their creative potential.
